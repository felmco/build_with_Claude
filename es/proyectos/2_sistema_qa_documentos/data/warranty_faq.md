# Warranty FAQ

## How long is the warranty?

All Acme electronics carry a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Accessories such as cables and cases carry a 90-day warranty.

## What does the warranty cover?

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized repairs.

## How do I make a claim?

Contact support with your order number and a short description or photo of the problem. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion, and replacements ship free of charge.
