# Acme Store Refund Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Exceptions

Opened software, gift cards, and perishable goods cannot be returned. Custom-engraved products are final sale unless they arrive damaged.

## How refunds are issued

Once the returned item reaches our warehouse, the refund is processed within 5 business days. Banks may take an additional 3 to 7 days to show the credit. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise a flat 6 dollar return label fee is deducted from the refund.
