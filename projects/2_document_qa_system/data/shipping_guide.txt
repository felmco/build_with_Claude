Acme Store Shipping Guide

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States and costs 5 dollars. Orders over 50 dollars ship free.

Express shipping delivers in 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern time ship the same day.

International shipping is available to Canada and the United Kingdom only. Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for customs duties and import taxes.

Every order receives a tracking number by email once it leaves the warehouse. If a package shows no movement for 5 days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.
